# Multiple Documents (Tabs)

MarkStudio Editor can have several documents open at once, each in its own tab, each with its own undo history and its own dirty/unsaved indicator.

- Click **+** next to the tab strip, or press **Ctrl+N**, to open a new tab.
- New untitled tabs are numbered so you can tell them apart: "Untitled", "Untitled 2", "Untitled 3", …
- Click a tab to switch to it — switching is instant, and each tab remembers its own scroll position and caret location.
- Click the **✕** on a tab to close it. If it has unsaved changes, you'll be asked whether to save first.
- Opening a file that's already open in another tab switches to that tab instead of opening a second copy.
