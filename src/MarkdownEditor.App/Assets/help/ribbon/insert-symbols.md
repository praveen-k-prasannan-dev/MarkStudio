# Insert Tab — Symbols

| Button | What it does |
|--------|---------------|
| 📅 Date | Inserts the current date/time as plain text |
| 😀 Emoji ▾ | Opens a small picker of common emoji (👍 ✅ ❌ ⚠️ 💡 📌 🔥 🎯 ❤️ 🚀, etc.) and inserts the one you click |

Emoji are inserted as literal Unicode characters, so they render identically in the editor, the preview, exported PDF/HTML, and exported Word documents.
