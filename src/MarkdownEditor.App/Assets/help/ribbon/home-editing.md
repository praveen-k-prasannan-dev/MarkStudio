# Home Tab — Editing

| Control | Shortcut | What it does |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| Find | `Ctrl+F` | Opens the find bar under the ribbon |
| Replace | `Ctrl+H` | Opens the find bar with the replace field focused |
| Select All | `Ctrl+A` | Selects the entire document |

## Find & Replace

`Ctrl+F` opens a bar just under the ribbon with a search box. `Ctrl+H` opens the same bar but also reveals a replace field.

- **Next / Previous** step through matches, wrapping around the start/end of the document.
- **Replace** replaces the current match; **Replace All** replaces every match and reports how many occurrences changed.
- **Match case** toggles case-sensitive matching.
- Press **Esc** to close the bar.
