# Home Tab — Clipboard

| Button | Shortcut | What it does |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Paste | `Ctrl+V` | Pastes text (or a dropped/pasted image, copied to an `assets/` folder next to your document) |
| Cut | `Ctrl+X` | Cuts the selection |
| Copy | `Ctrl+C` | Copies the selection |
| Undo | `Ctrl+Z` | Steps back through your edit history |
| Redo | `Ctrl+Y` | Re-applies an undone edit |
| **Copy as HTML** | — | Copies your selection (or the whole document) as real formatted HTML |

## Copy as HTML

This is the one worth knowing about: instead of copying raw Markdown characters (`**bold**`, `# Heading`), **Copy as HTML** renders your selection through the same engine as the live preview and puts *actual formatted HTML* on the clipboard (plus a plain-text fallback). Paste it into Word, Outlook, or a browser's rich-text field and the formatting carries over — bold stays bold, headings stay headings, tables stay tables.
