MarkStudio Editor bundles the following third-party JavaScript libraries to render
Mermaid diagrams and mathematical notation locally in the preview pane, with no
network access required at runtime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mermaid (mermaid.min.js)
https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid
Licensed under the MIT License.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MathJax (mathjax-tex-svg.js)
https://github.com/mathjax/MathJax
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MarkStudio Editor also bundles the following for offline spell checking:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WeCantSpell.Hunspell (a pure .NET Hunspell-compatible spell checking library)
https://github.com/aardappel/WeCantSpell.Hunspell (fork of NHunspell/Hunspell logic)
Licensed under the MIT License.

en_US Hunspell dictionary (Assets/dictionaries/en_US.dic, en_US.aff)
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries (en/ folder), derived from SCOWL
http://wordlist.sourceforge.net

permissive attribution license (see the SCOWL/en_US dictionary README for
full text); redistribution with attribution is permitted.
